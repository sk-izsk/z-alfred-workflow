# Background Items for Alfred 6

See and control what starts at login or runs in the background: login items, launch agents and
daemons, with the app each belongs to, whether it is running, and leftovers whose app is gone.

Requires macOS 13+, Alfred 6 with Powerpack and Node.js 22+ or Bun. Nothing else to install.

## Open

Type `bgitems`, or choose Background Items in Swiss. Leftovers (the program no longer exists, usually
from an uninstalled app) come first with an orange icon, then login items, your launch agents, agents
for all users and daemons. Running items have a green icon and show their process ID. Pick **All
Items**, **Login Items**, **Launch Agents**, **Daemons** or **Leftovers** above the list, or type to
filter by name, app or label.

## Actions

- **↩** reveals the item's plist (or a login item's app) in Finder.
- **⌘S** stops or starts it, **⌘D** disables or enables it (a disabled item won't start again at login
  or restart), **⌘O** opens the plist, **⌘C** copies its label.
- **⌘K** also has Remove from Login Items, Move Plist to Trash (your own agents only), Open Login
  Items Settings, Refresh and Configuration. Removing and trashing ask first.
- Changing a daemon (or an agent for all users installed by an administrator) shows macOS's own
  password dialog.
- **Back** (Hyper+B) returns to Swiss when opened there, otherwise to Alfred's search.

## How it works

- Launch agents and daemons come from their folders and `launchctl`, without administrator rights.
- Login items come from System Events: macOS asks once whether Alfred may control it. If you decline,
  everything else still shows.
- The list loads in about half a second.
